# J4.6 / J5.11: live playable-route geometry

The online decision must use the selected-Tee CoursePrep polyline for route length, hazard
stations and landing coordinates. A current position starts at its nearest route projection;
water behind that station cannot constrain the next shot. Green windows use the same origin.

The product tests combine hand-calculated synthetic geometry with the sanitized real release
31834/2 in `tests/product/fixtures/geometry/31834-hole2`. Its HTTP regression uses real loaders,
authority binding and meshes: Driver 197 + 8I 121 finishes at station 318 inside the selected green;
water is 50.5–84.2 m. The synthetic diagonal shortcut crosses a second lake while its playable
dogleg does not. A straight-route control retains the Driver 240 + 8I 160 finish at station 400.
