## P2: absence from the phone shot set does not establish capture order

`WatchRoundModel.distanceFromLatestShotM` line475 chooses any Watch location
absent from the phone shot set ahead of the phone's last-shot coordinates.
On reconnect, an older undelivered Watch mark can therefore override a newer
phone mark until the Watch event is relayed. The phone's timestamp sort cannot
resolve this intermediate state: the snapshot has coordinates but no origin
timestamp. Concrete code-analysis case, not an executed Swift/device probe:
Watch tee10:00 at40.0/116, phone second10:05 at40.001/116, current40.002/116;
the method chooses the tee (~222m) instead of the newer second shot (~111m).

Requested capture identity/time alongside phone coordinates, newest same-hole
origin selection by capture time, and real receive/record/relaunch regressions
for an older delayed Watch shot, a genuinely newer Watch shot and other holes.
The existing monotonic snapshot guard must remain.

The first published comment also claimed an upload-acknowledgement origin
lifetime bug. Further call-site tracing showed round-store `markPosted` runs
on finish; live phone acknowledgements clear a separate transport queue.
That unsupported live-path finding and requested persistence change were
removed, explicitly explained in the edited comment, and retained verbatim
in `review-first-published.md` for the audit record.
