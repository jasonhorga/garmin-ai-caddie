## Interface after the revert

`python ops/agenthub_deploy.py up` goes back to its pre-449 output. Failure
lines are `result=failed action=up part=<part> container=<name|none>
elapsed_s=<n.n> reason=<free text>`, as card 447/448 defined them. A held sync
lock reports text that contains `sync lock`. A release tie names the tied
containers inside the reason text. There is no `reason=<code>` vocabulary, no
`candidates=` field and no `up_line` helper. The `deploy` and `rollback`
subcommands are unchanged by #449 and by this revert.
